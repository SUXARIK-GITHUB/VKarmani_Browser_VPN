IMPORTANT BROWSER BOUNDARY
A normal unpacked Chromium extension cannot register its own Native Messaging host or silently self-install. Therefore one local double-click of 00_INSTALL_VKARMANI.cmd plus the browser's Load unpacked action is the minimum safe/manual bootstrap for this architecture.
